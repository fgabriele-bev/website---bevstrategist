2. Who this notice applies to

This notice applies to website visitors, people who contact us, prospective and current clients, and owners, employees, representatives and professional contacts of producers and other organisations with which we work.

3. Personal data we collect

Depending on our relationship with you, we may collect:

  • your name, role and contact details;
  • information about the organisation you represent;
  • correspondence and records of meetings or calls;
  • information provided through questionnaires;
  • information required to evaluate or provide our services;
  • contractual, billing and payment information; and
  • limited technical information recorded by our website hosting provider.

We do not ask you to provide special-category personal data. Please do not provide it unless we have specifically agreed that it is necessary.

4. How we receive personal data

We may receive personal data directly from you, from the organisation you represent, from another person involved in a project or business relationship, or from professional directories, company websites and other publicly available business sources.

5. How and why we use personal data

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, assess whether our services may be appropriate, prepare proposals and contracts, provide and manage our services, communicate with clients and their representatives, maintain business and financial records, comply with legal obligations, protect our legal rights and send relevant professional communications where permitted by law.

We rely on one or more of the following legal bases: taking steps before entering into a contract; performing a contract; complying with a legal obligation; our legitimate interests in operating, protecting and developing our business; and consent where specifically required.

You may object at any time to receiving marketing or promotional communications.

6. Sharing personal data

We share personal data only where necessary. Recipients may include providers of email, cloud storage, questionnaires, customer relationship management and business administration services; accountants, legal advisers and other professional advisers; contractors supporting our business under appropriate confidentiality obligations; and public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Where our services include introducing a producer to importers, distributors, buyers or other trade contacts in the United Kingdom, we share the business contact details needed for that introduction.

We do not sell personal data.

7. International transfers

Some technology providers may process personal data outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we use providers and contractual arrangements intended to provide an appropriate level of protection in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

8. How long we retain personal data

  • Contractual, administrative and financial records are retained for the periods required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Information relating to a contact who does not become a client is normally retained for no longer than 24 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Records of objections to marketing communications may be retained for as long as necessary to ensure that the objection continues to be respected.

Information may be retained for longer where required by law or in connection with an actual or potential legal dispute.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access is limited to those who require the information for legitimate business purposes.

10. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law and the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data; request restriction of processing; object to processing; request the transfer of certain data; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions.

To exercise a right, contact fgabriele@bevstrategist.co.uk.

11. Automated decisions

We do not use personal data to make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a concern about how we use your personal data. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

13. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, systems or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will always be shown at the top of the notice.
